PocketQwen Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

PocketQwen is designed to run its language model locally on your Android device.

On-device information

Chat prompts and generated responses are processed by the bundled local model. Conversation history and app preferences may be stored in the app's private storage. An account is not required for offline inference.

Optional online features

If Web Search is enabled, the search query is sent to the configured search provider. If you submit a report, the category, details, selected excerpt, and app version are sent to the configured report service. Ordinary offline chat is not sent to a cloud AI model.

Retention and deletion

Local chats can be deleted in the app. Uninstalling removes app-private data subject to Android backup settings. Report retention depends on the configured receiving service and must be published before the report feature is enabled.

Age and safety

PocketQwen is intended for people aged 13 and older. Model outputs can be inaccurate and should not replace qualified professional advice.

Contact

Privacy contact details must be added by the publisher before public release. This policy must be reviewed against the final app behaviour and Google Play Data safety declarations.
