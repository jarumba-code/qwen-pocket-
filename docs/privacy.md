# PocketQwen Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 4 November 2026

PocketQwen is designed to run its language model locally on your Android device.

## Information processed on your device
- Chat messages and generated answers are processed by the bundled on-device language model.
- Conversation history and app preferences may be stored in the app's private storage so the app can restore your chats and settings.
- The app does not need an account to run local inference.

## Information sent outside the device
Ordinary prompts and model responses are not sent to a cloud AI provider for inference.

If you voluntarily submit a feedback or safety report, the app may send the specific response excerpt you select, the report category, app version, and optional details to the report service. Do not include passwords, private keys, financial account details, or other sensitive information in a report.

## Data retention and deletion
Local conversations can be deleted from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes its private local data, subject to Android backup settings. Report data, if received by the service, will be retained only as long as necessary to investigate and improve the app; the final retention period will be published before the report feature is enabled.

## Children and age rating
PocketQwen is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not designed for children under 13.

## Security and limitations
Local storage is protected by Android's app sandbox, but no software can guarantee absolute security. Model outputs can be inaccurate. Do not rely on PocketQwen as a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, or emergency advice.

## Contact
Privacy questions: create a private support request through the app's official GitHub repository.

This policy is a launch draft. Replace the contact section with a monitored support email or website contact channel before public release, and update this policy to match the final app behaviour and Google Play Data safety declarations.
