WHERE THE ANSWER COMES FROM

Your phone handles
ordinary chat.

For standard conversations, the app uses its included AI model on your Android device. Your prompt is processed locally rather than sent to a cloud AI chat service.

⌂

Local conversations

After installation and setup, ordinary chats can work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

▤

Conversation history

Your conversations are kept within the app on your device so you can return to them.
