PocketQwen is an Android project exploring practical on-device AI. Its bundled Qwen model runs locally, so everyday chats do not need cloud inference.

Built for control

Keep ordinary conversations on your device. Optional online features, when enabled, are separate and clearly identified. Local model answers can be inaccurate and should be checked.
