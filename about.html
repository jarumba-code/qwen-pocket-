ABOUT POCKETQWEN

AI that feels
closer to home.

PocketQwen is an independent Android project exploring a more personal, on-device way to use AI.

THE IDEA

Useful first.
Honest always.

AI should be approachable. It should also be clear about what it can do, where it has limits and when an internet connection is involved.

WHAT GUIDES THE PROJECT

A few simple principles.

touch_app

Approachable by design

A familiar chat experience that does not require technical knowledge to get started.

smartphone

Keep chat close

Ordinary conversations are processed on the device rather than sent to a cloud AI chat service.

visibility

Be clear

Describe current features honestly and distinguish them from things still being developed.

tune

Respect user choice

Keep online capabilities separate so their use can be explained clearly.

INDEPENDENT PROJECT

Built around a practical idea.

PocketQwen is not presented as a replacement for professional advice or a guarantee of perfect answers. It is an assistant for exploring ideas, learning and everyday tasks. Always check important information independently.
