2. What EKIP does

EKIP is an Android AI assistant that includes a language model for on-device inference. Ordinary chat prompts and responses are processed locally by the model; the chat engine does not require cloud AI inference. Conversation history, local memory notes, and app preferences are stored in app-private storage on your device.

3. Optional online features

Web Search requires an internet connection. It may be used when you enable the feature or when the app recognises a request for current information. When a search is made, the current query is sent to EKIP's hosted search endpoint and its configured search provider. Search results may then be supplied to the local model as reference context. The full conversation history and local memory are not sent as the search query.

Online features depend on third-party services and may be unavailable, changed, or discontinued. Do not include passwords, private keys, recovery phrases, payment credentials, or other secrets in search queries.
