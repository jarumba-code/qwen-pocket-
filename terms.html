1. What PocketQwen does

PocketQwen is an AI assistant designed to run its language model locally on your device. Normal chat prompts and responses are processed by the on-device model and are not sent to a cloud inference service by the chat engine.

2. Optional online features

Features such as Web Search require an internet connection. When Web Search is manually enabled or the app detects a time-sensitive query, the search query is sent to PocketQwen's hosted search endpoint and its configured search provider. Search excerpts and source links may then be supplied to the local model so it can prepare an answer. Do not include passwords, private keys, recovery phrases, payment details or other secrets in search queries.
