POCKETQWEN · PRIVACY

Privacy, in plain language.

This page explains what happens to your information when you use PocketQwen.

Launch review required. This policy must be checked against the final app release, deployed services, report-retention arrangements and the Google Play Data safety form before public launch.

Effective date: 5 November 2026

1. Local conversations

PocketQwen uses a bundled Qwen language model for ordinary chat. Prompts and responses are processed by the model on your Android device; ordinary local inference does not require a cloud AI service. Conversation history and app preferences are stored in app-private storage on the device.

2. Optional online features

Online features are separate from local inference. Web Search is under development and is not available in the current Android build. When an online search feature is released, the app is intended to send the search query—not the full conversation history—to the configured search provider. The app should clearly identify the online action and show its returned sources.

A report-submission endpoint exists in the project backend, but its availability depends on deployment configuration. If report submission is enabled in a future build, the report category, optional details, selected response excerpt and app version may be sent to the configured report service. Do not submit passwords, private keys, payment credentials or other sensitive information.

3. Data storage and deletion

Local conversations and preferences are stored by the app on your device. You can delete local conversations through the app's available controls. Data sent to a third-party online provider is subject to that provider's own policies. Before any report service is enabled publicly, the operator must publish its retention and deletion arrangements.

4. Diagnostics and service providers

This policy does not claim that every device-level diagnostic or platform event is disabled. The final release should be reviewed to identify any analytics, crash reporting, hosting logs, search-provider processing or report-webhook processing and describe them accurately here.

5. Age and important limitations

PocketQwen is intended for users aged 13 and older. Model output can be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. It is not a substitute for qualified medical, legal, financial or emergency assistance.

6. Contact and support

For questions or to report an issue, visit the support contact details that will be published on the PocketQwen website before public launch. A monitored privacy contact address should be published before public launch.
