POCKETQWEN · PRIVACY

Privacy, in
plain language.

This page explains how PocketQwen is designed to handle information. It must be checked against the final public app and services before launch.

infoLaunch review notice

The public release has not been published yet. This policy is a working description and needs a final technical and legal review before public distribution.

Effective date: 5 November 2026

1. Local conversations

PocketQwen uses a model included with the Android app for ordinary chat. Prompts and responses are processed on your device. Local inference does not require sending the conversation to a cloud AI chat service. Conversation history and app preferences are stored in app-private storage on the device.

2. Online features

Web Search is under development and is not available in the current Android build. If it is released, a search query may be sent to the configured search provider to retrieve current information. The app should clearly identify that online action before it happens.

3. Reports and feedback

A report-submission endpoint exists in the project backend, but whether it is enabled depends on deployment configuration. If report submission is enabled in a future build, the information shown in the report form will be sent to the configured service. Avoid including passwords, private keys, payment details or other sensitive information.

4. Storage and deletion

Local conversations and preferences are stored by the app on your device. Use the app's available controls to delete local conversation data. Information sent to an online provider may also be subject to that provider's own retention and deletion policies.

5. Diagnostics and service providers

This policy does not claim that all device-level diagnostics, crash reports or hosting logs are disabled. Before public release, the final app and its services must be reviewed so any analytics, diagnostics and third-party processing can be described accurately.

6. Important limitations

AI responses can be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. Do not rely on PocketQwen as a substitute for qualified medical, legal, financial or emergency assistance. Check important information independently.

7. Contact

A monitored privacy contact address has not yet been published. Contact details should be added here before the public release.
