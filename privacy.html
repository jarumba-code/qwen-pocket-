Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Launch draft: This policy must be reviewed against the final release, deployed endpoints, retention arrangements and Google Play Data safety form before public launch.

Local processing

  • Ordinary chat prompts and model responses are processed by the bundled Qwen model on your Android device.
  • Conversation history and preferences are stored in app-private storage.
  • Local inference does not require an account or cloud AI service.

Optional online features

If Web Search is enabled, the search query is sent to the configured search service. Search results are returned to the app for use as reference context. The app should show the source links and make clear that search is online.

If you submit a report, the report category, optional details, selected response excerpt and app version are sent to the configured report intake service. Do not submit passwords, private keys, payment credentials or other sensitive information.

Storage and deletion

Local conversations can be deleted in the app. The current app build disables Android backup. Report submissions are forwarded to a configured service; retention and deletion terms must be documented by the operator of that service before the feature is enabled.

Age and safety

PocketQwen is intended for users aged 13 and older. Model output can be inaccurate and is not a substitute for qualified medical, legal, financial or emergency assistance.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the project repository's issue/support process. A monitored support contact should be published before the app is publicly released.
