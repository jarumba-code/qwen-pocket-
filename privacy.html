Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information EKIP handles, what stays on your Android device, and what may leave the device when online search is used.

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Applies to EKIP Android preview version 1.1.0 and this website.

1. Who operates EKIP

EKIP is an independent Android software project maintained by JIMM.tech in Nigeria. This policy describes the app's current implementation and its configured online services. Features and service providers may change; this page will be updated when material changes are made.

2. Information kept on your device

EKIP includes a local language model. During ordinary local chat, your prompts are processed by that model on your Android device. The chat engine does not send your conversation to a cloud AI model for inference.

Conversation history, local memory notes, your optional profile name, and app preferences are stored in the app's private storage on your device. EKIP does not require you to create an account to use local chat.

Local information remains on the device unless you choose to share it through an online feature, or use Android/device tools that expose it. Clearing EKIP's app data or uninstalling the app can remove locally stored information.

3. Optional web search

EKIP can use online search for current information. A search may be sent when you enable Web Search, or when the app recognises a query as asking for time-sensitive information—for example, a request about current news, weather, prices, exchange rates, or the latest version of something. You can ask the app not to search, such as by saying “do not search” or “offline only”.

When a search is made, the app sends the current search query—not your entire conversation history or local memory—to the configured EKIP search endpoint at https://qwen-pocket.vercel.app/api/search. The service forwards that query to Tavily, a third-party search provider, and returns a small set of result titles, links, and excerpts. Those excerpts may be supplied to the local model as context. The response itself is generated on your device.

Online search requires an internet connection. The request may be processed by the hosting provider and search provider, including ordinary network and service metadata such as IP address, request time, and the query needed to perform the search. Their own privacy and retention terms also apply. If the search service is unavailable, the app may answer using only its local model.

4. Website and hosting

This policy and the EKIP website are served as static website files. The hosting provider may process standard web-request information for delivery, security, and reliability. Other pages on the website may load fonts or interface resources from third-party content delivery services, which receive the network information needed to provide those resources.

5. Reports, analytics, and advertising

Ordinary local chat is not automatically submitted to a feedback or report service. The project repository contains a report-intake endpoint, but that endpoint only accepts submissions when it is configured and called; it is not part of the normal local-chat generation path.

This policy does not claim that Android, device manufacturers, app distribution platforms, or website and hosting providers collect no diagnostics or operational logs. Review the relevant provider policies for their handling of that information.

6. Data sharing and retention

EKIP does not operate a cloud account or conversation-sync service in the current app. Local conversations and preferences stay in app-private storage unless you choose to share information through an online feature.

Search queries are sent to the services described above. Their processing and retention are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. EKIP's project maintainer does not provide a control to delete information from those providers' systems.

7. Your choices

  • Use ordinary local chat without enabling Web Search. Note that the app may still recognise certain current-information queries and initiate a search; say “do not search” or “offline only” to suppress that search for a prompt.
  • Do not put passwords, private keys, recovery phrases, payment details, or other secrets into search queries.
  • Delete conversations in the app where the available conversation controls permit, or clear EKIP's app data/uninstall through Android settings to remove local app data.
  • Use provider privacy controls or contact the relevant provider for requests about data it processes.

8. Children and age

EKIP is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not designed for children under 13, and the project does not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13.

9. AI limitations

AI responses can be inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated. Do not treat EKIP as a substitute for qualified medical, legal, financial, or emergency assistance. Check important information independently.

10. Contact and policy updates

For general project questions, use the EKIP project issue tracker. It is a public channel: do not post private information, passwords, account details, or sensitive personal data there. A dedicated private privacy-contact address has not yet been published; that contact method should be added before broad public distribution.

When this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be published on this page.
